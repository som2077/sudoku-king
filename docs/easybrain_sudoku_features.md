# Sudoku.com - Classic Sudoku by Easybrain: Features & UX Analysis

## 1. Introduction
**Sudoku.com - Classic Sudoku**, developed by Easybrain, is one of the most popular and highly rated Sudoku mobile apps on the Google Play Store. It is renowned for blending the classic pen-and-paper puzzle experience with modern digital conveniences. The app caters to a vast audience, from absolute beginners learning the ropes to seasoned masters seeking complex logical challenges.

## 2. Gameplay Mechanics
The app faithfully reproduces traditional Sudoku while incorporating intuitive digital mechanics designed to streamline the solving process:
* **Notes (Pencil Mode):** Replicates writing small numbers in the margins. It allows players to make notes for potential candidates in empty cells. As numbers are placed on the board, these notes are automatically updated to prevent confusion.
* **Hints:** A built-in guidance system that does more than just give the answer; it explains the logic behind a move, helping players learn and improve their strategies when stuck.
* **Auto-Check:** An optional toggle that immediately highlights errors as they are made, offering real-time feedback. Players seeking a purist experience can turn this off to manually spot mistakes.
* **Undo & Eraser:** Features unlimited undo steps and a dedicated eraser, allowing players to backtrack easily and providing a stress-free environment for experimentation.
* **Number-First Input:** A quality-of-life mechanic where a player can select a number first and tap multiple cells to lock it in, significantly speeding up gameplay compared to selecting a cell and then picking a number.

## 3. Difficulty Levels
The game is praised for its meticulously balanced progression system, which is determined by the complexity of the logical techniques required rather than just the number of pre-filled clues:
* **Six Tiers of Difficulty:** The app typically includes levels such as **Fast, Easy, Medium, Hard, Expert,** and occasionally **Giant** or **Master**.
* **Beginner Friendly:** The "Easy" and "Fast" levels rely on fundamental scanning and "singles" techniques, making it highly accessible.
* **Master Challenges:** The "Expert" and "Giant" levels require players to employ advanced Sudoku strategies (e.g., X-Wings, Swordfish, and complex chaining deductions), ensuring that high-level players are genuinely challenged.

## 4. UI/UX Elements
Easybrain has prioritized a clean, distraction-free environment that minimizes visual fatigue during long play sessions:
* **Minimalist Design:** The grid is presented with sharp, clear contrast. Grid lines and 3x3 block separators are distinctly defined.
* **Smart Highlighting:** Selecting a cell automatically highlights the corresponding row, column, and block. Furthermore, the app highlights duplicate numbers to immediately flag invalid placements.
* **Themes & Accessibility:** Offers multiple color schemes, importantly including a comfortable **Dark Mode** for nighttime play or light sensitivity, reducing eye strain.
* **Responsive Grid:** The UI scales well across various screen sizes (both phones and tablets) and offers seamless interaction with both touch and stylus inputs.

## 5. Gamification and Engagement
To encourage daily retention and long-term play, the app includes several robust gamification features:
* **Daily Challenges:** A unique, fresh puzzle is presented every day. Completing these allows users to earn unique trophies. 
* **Puzzle Archive:** A calendar interface allows users to go back and play past daily challenges they might have missed.
* **Seasonal Events:** The app frequently hosts time-limited seasonal events where players must complete specific puzzle sets to win exclusive medals.
* **Tournaments:** Periodic competitive modes where players can race against the clock to climb global leaderboards.
* **Comprehensive Statistics:** The app tracks deep metrics for every difficulty tier, including games played, games won, best times, average times, and current/longest win streaks.

## 6. Unique Selling Points (USPs)
* **Digital Convenience with Analog Feel:** It strikes the perfect balance of feeling like a classic puzzle book while eliminating the tediousness of erasing mistakes and updating manual notes.
* **Adaptive Learning:** The hint system serves as an in-game tutor, making the app an excellent tool for users looking to actually get better at Sudoku, not just pass the time.
* **Cross-Platform Consistency:** Available on mobile and via web browser, ensuring users can get their Sudoku fix anywhere.
* **Offline Playability:** Players can enjoy the vast majority of the app's features and puzzles without an active internet connection, making it an ideal travel companion.
